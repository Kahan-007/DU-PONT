How to read the DuPont analysis
Tax burden
Net income ÷ earnings before tax. It shows the share of pre-tax earnings that becomes net income.
Interest burden
Earnings before tax ÷ EBIT. It shows how much operating profit remains before tax.
Operating margin
EBIT ÷ revenue. It measures operating profit per rupee of sales.
Asset turnover
Revenue ÷ average assets. It measures the sales generated per rupee of assets.
Equity multiplier
Average assets ÷ average equity. A higher multiple indicates more assets per rupee of equity.
Return on equity (ROE)
The product of the five factors. See the company details to learn what drove its verdict and whether period-end balances were used.
