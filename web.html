How to use the screener

Explore how operating performance, financing, and tax combine to produce return on equity.

Overview
Switch chart views to explore margin, turnover, leverage, and interest burden. Select a dot to open a company or select a sector or verdict bar to filter the dashboard.
Companies
Search by name or ticker, sort ratio columns, and open a company for benchmarks, financial inputs, and source links.
Compare
Choose two companies to compare the five DuPont factors and ROE. Check the reporting periods before interpreting differences.
Data and offline access
Sector benchmarks use comparable reporting periods when available. The offline download includes the data from the version you save.
